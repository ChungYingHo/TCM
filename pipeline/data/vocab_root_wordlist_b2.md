## 書頁 81（IMG_1696）　主題：表示「新」與「第一」的字根

### neo (nov): new

1. **neoclassical** (a.) 新古典的
   - This neoclassical building evokes Roman churches.
2. **innovation** (n.) 創新，革新
   - 【衍】innovate (v.) 創新，革新、innovative (a.) 創新的
   - This notebook employs the latest innovations in voice recognition.
   - ※ 課本印「格新」，高解析放大確認過。「格新」非中文詞，正確是「革新」，此處用正確寫法，屬課本錯字。
3. **renovate** (v.) 翻修，整建
   - 【衍】renovation (n.) 翻修，整建
   - The man is taking out a bank loan to renovate his house.
4. **novel** (a., n.) 新奇的；小說
   - His suggestion provides a novel approach to the problem.
   - The latest novel of the best-selling writer is a mystery.
5. **novice** (n.) 新手，生手
   - Although a novice in the field, I can learn very fast.

### prim: first

6. **primary** (a., n.) 首要的，主要的；黨內初選
   - The primary responsibility of a manager is to lead her subordinates.
   - This cartoon uses mostly the three primary colors.
   - The winner in the primaries will be nominated as Presidential candidate.
7. **premier** (n., a.) 總理；首屈一指的
   - The French Premier and the British Prime Minister are having a summit soon.
   - The man is one of the world's premier (or premiere) chefs.
8. **premiere** (n., v., a.) 首演；首屈一指的
   - I'm going to the world premiere of the new movie.
   - The new movie is going to premiere tonight.
