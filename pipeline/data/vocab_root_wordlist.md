# 第二部分 依字根排列 — 固定字清單（主 agent 逐張照片轉錄）

規則：此清單是查證 worker 的權威輸入，**worker 只能驗證、不得增刪改字**。
例句一律照抄課本；中文取課本。

課本在字根之上還有一層主題標題（如「表示『位置』的字根」），一併記錄。

## 書頁 67（IMG_1682）　主題：表示「位置」的字根

### loc: place

1. **location** (n.) 地點，位置
   - 【衍】locate (v.) 找到，座落
   - The dentist is still trying to locate the nerve.
   - The house is located near a river.
   - This house has a convenient location.
2. **allocate** (v.) 分配，分派
   - 【衍】allocation (n.) 分配，分派
   - As General Manager, you must see to it that company resources are allocated equally.
3. **locomotion** (n.) 位移，移動
   - 【衍】motion (n.) 動作
   - Some animals achieve locomotion by changing body shape.
4. **locomotive** (n.) 火車頭
   - The stock market and the real-estate market are the twin locomotives of a nation's economy.
5. **relocate** (v.) 重新安置，搬遷
   - 【衍】relocation (n.) 重新安置，搬遷
   - He went to see a doctor to relocate his dislocated shoulder.
   - The company has relocated to Shanghai.

### centr: center

6. **concentrate** (v., n.) 集中；濃縮液
   - 【衍】concentration (n.) 集中，濃度
   - It's difficult to concentrate on the reading, with so much noise around.
   - The bottled juice was restored from juice concentrates.
7. **concentric** (a.) 同圓心的
   - The shooting target is composed of a series of concentric circles.

### circum (circul): circle, around

10. **circumference** (n.) 圓周，周邊
    - Ancient Egyptians calculated the circumference of the globe by measuring shadows.

    - 【衍】circuitous (a.) 迂迴的，曲折的
    - The management consultant is a familiar figure on the lecture circuit.
    - This factory manufactures circuit boards for radios.
12. **circulation** (n.) 循環，流通，發行量
    - 【衍】circulate (v.) 循環，流通
    - High cholesterol tends to impede blood circulation.
    - There are a lot of counterfeit 20-dollar bills in circulation.
    - This newspaper has a circulation of over one million.
13. **circumspect** (a.) 謹慎的，小心的
    - 【衍】circumspection (n.) 謹慎，小心
    - The man has been successful because he is always circumspect in his dealings.
14. **circumvent** (v.) 規避
    - 【衍】circumvention (n.) 規避
    - The businessman circumvented government regulations by registering his company with a foreign country.
