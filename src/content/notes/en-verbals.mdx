當一個動詞後面要再接「另一個動作」時，到底用**動名詞（V-ing）**還是**不定詞（to V）**？這要靠**記動詞分群**＋**抓固定句型**，是克漏字與文法題的高頻點。

## 一、只接動名詞（V-ing）的動詞

| 動詞群 | 例句 |
|---|---|
| enjoy, finish, mind, avoid | She **avoided answering** the question. |
| suggest, consider, imagine | He **considered changing** his diet. |
| admit, deny, practice, risk | The patient **denied taking** any drugs. |
| keep, give up, can't help | I **can't help worrying** about the result. |

> 串記口訣：「**避免承認練習想像，介意放棄忍不住**」——這些後面都接 V-ing。

<GerundInfinitivePicker client:visible />

## 二、只接不定詞（to V）的動詞

| 動詞群 | 例句 |
|---|---|
| want, hope, wish, expect | They **hope to find** a cure. |
| decide, plan, agree, refuse | She **refused to sign** the consent form. |
| promise, manage, afford, offer | He **managed to recover** quickly. |
| ask, tell, advise, allow + 受詞 | The doctor **advised her to rest**. |

## 三、固定句型：to 其實是介系詞 → 接 V-ing

下列的 **to 是介系詞**，後面要接 V-ing（不是原形）：

- be used to + **V-ing**（習慣於）：I am used to **getting** up early.
- look forward to + **V-ing**：I look forward to **hearing** from you.
- object to / be devoted to / be opposed to + **V-ing**

> 對照陷阱：**used to + 原形 V**（過去常常）≠ **be used to + V-ing**（習慣於）。

## 四、介系詞後一律 V-ing

in / on / by / before / after / without / for + **V-ing**：

- You can stay healthy **by exercising** regularly.
- He left **without saying** goodbye.

## 五、意義會改變的動詞（最常考）

| 動詞 | + to V | + V-ing |
|---|---|---|
| **stop** | 停下來去做（目的） | 停止做（停掉這件事） |
| **remember / forget** | （未來）記得／忘記要做 | （過去）記得／忘記做過 |
| **try** | 努力去做 | 試試看某方法 |
| **regret** | 遺憾要（通知壞消息） | 後悔做過 |

- He **stopped to smoke**.（停下腳步去抽菸）／He **stopped smoking**.（戒菸）
- Remember **to lock** the door.（還沒鎖）／I remember **locking** the door.（鎖過了）

<Memorize>
只接 V-ing：<b>enjoy / avoid / finish / mind / suggest / consider / admit / deny / practice</b>。<br />
只接 to V：<b>want / hope / decide / plan / agree / refuse / promise / manage / afford</b>。<br />
<b>be used to / look forward to + V-ing</b>（to 是介系詞）；介系詞後一律 V-ing。<br />
<b>stop to V</b>（停下去做）≠ <b>stop V-ing</b>（停止做）；<b>remember to V</b>（沒做）≠ <b>remember V-ing</b>（做過）。
</Memorize>

<ExampleQuestion client:visible
  n={1}
  q="The surgeon suggested ____ the operation until the patient was stronger."
  options={["to postpone", "postponing", "postpone", "postponed"]}
  answer="(B) postponing"
  steps={[
    "<b>suggest</b> 後面接<b>動名詞（V-ing）</b>，不接 to V。",
    "（注意：suggest 若帶 that 子句則用原形——suggested that it be postponed，但本題空格直接接動詞。）",
    "答案：<b>postponing</b>。"
  ]}
/>

<ExampleQuestion client:visible
  n={2}
  q="After years in the ward, the nurse got used to ____ on her feet all day."
  options={["be", "being", "been", "to be"]}
  answer="(B) being"
  steps={[
    "<b>be / get used to</b> 的 <b>to 是介系詞</b>，後面接 <b>V-ing</b>（習慣於）。",
    "別跟 used to V（過去常常）搞混。",
    "答案：<b>being</b>。"
  ]}
/>

<ExampleQuestion client:visible
  n={3}

  ]}
/>
